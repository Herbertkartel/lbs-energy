Solar Irrigation & Water Pumping

Solar pumping systems for farms that need water for crop irrigation, livestock and other agricultural activities. We consider water source, required flow, pumping head, irrigation method, site conditions and daily demand before shaping a system.

  • Farm irrigation system planning
  • Water supply for livestock and farm use
Business energy

Commercial & Business Solar

Solar power options for businesses that want to improve energy reliability and manage operating costs. System design depends on the customer's load profile, operating hours, backup needs and site conditions.

  • Systems for agro-processing businesses
  • Backup and hybrid power options where suitable

Financing and affordability

Explore payment options without confusing who provides the finance.

Where suitable independent financing partners are available, we can help customers explore equipment financing or flexible payment arrangements. Any credit or pay-over-time facility is offered and approved by the finance partner, not by LBS Energy. Availability, eligibility and terms depend on that partner.

Our role

We understand the project, coordinate supplier and installer options, and help the customer navigate the project. We do not present ourselves as a lender or promise financing approval.
