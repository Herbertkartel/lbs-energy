About LBS Energy

We connect energy needs with practical project solutions.

LBS Energy is focused on productive-use solar solutions in Uganda. Our starting point is not a catalogue of equipment; it is understanding what a farm or business needs to power and what a suitable system must deliver.

We give particular attention to solar irrigation and water pumping, alongside commercial solar power and selected productive-use equipment for processing and storage.

We work through supplier and technical partnerships, helping customers define requirements, compare options and coordinate project delivery.
