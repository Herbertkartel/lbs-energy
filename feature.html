Our business model

Secure the project first. Source the equipment when it's needed.

LBS Energy operates as a solar solutions and project-coordination business. We focus on customer acquisition, needs assessment, solution development and project delivery. We do not need to hold large stocks of panels, pumps, batteries or other equipment before a customer is ready to proceed.

Once a customer confirms a project and the commercial terms are agreed, we work with suitable suppliers and installation partners to fulfil the order.

How LBS creates value

  • Customer discovery and energy-needs assessment
  • System scoping and supplier quotations
  • Procurement coordination for confirmed projects
  • Installation coordination and customer handover
  • After-sales support and maintenance coordination
Project journey

From first conversation to working system.

Each project moves through clear steps, with the customer agreeing the scope and cost before equipment is ordered.

01

Understand the need

We discuss the operation, location, energy use, water demand and desired outcome.

02

Scope and quote

We develop the project requirements and obtain suitable equipment and installation quotations.

03

Confirm and source

After the customer accepts the proposal and payment terms are agreed, equipment is sourced for that project.

04

Coordinate delivery

We coordinate with technical partners for installation, commissioning and customer handover.

How LBS earns revenue

Depending on the project agreement, revenue may come from an equipment supply margin, project development or coordination fees, installation coordination fees and after-sales or maintenance arrangements. These are agreed transparently within each project quotation.

Flexible payment pathways

Where an independent finance partner offers a suitable facility, customers may be referred to explore asset finance or pay-over-time options. The finance partner assesses the customer, approves the facility and, where its arrangement allows, pays the equipment supplier directly. LBS Energy does not lend its own capital, carry customer credit risk or guarantee approval. Any referral or project fee is agreed with the relevant partner and disclosed.
