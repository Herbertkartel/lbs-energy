Our core solutions

A focused range of solar solutions.

We concentrate on energy systems that help customers produce, process, store and run their businesses. Every project starts with understanding the work to be powered.

Solar Irrigation & Water Pumping

Solar-powered pumping options for crop irrigation, livestock water and other farm water needs. We help assess water demand, pumping requirements and suitable system options.

Commercial & Business Solar

Solar power systems for shops, offices, workshops, agro-processors and other businesses seeking to manage energy costs and improve power reliability.

Productive-Use Equipment

Solar energy solutions for selected equipment such as milling, agro-processing and cold storage, assessed according to the customer's operating needs.

How we work

You don't need to buy equipment before you have a project.

LBS Energy uses a demand-first, partner-led model. We work with you to define the system, obtain quotations from suitable suppliers and coordinate qualified technical partners for delivery. Equipment is sourced for confirmed customer projects rather than held as inventory.

Understand your energy need before recommending equipment.
Compare supplier options and project costs.
Coordinate procurement and installation with project partners.
Explore independent financing partners where available and subject to their approval.
