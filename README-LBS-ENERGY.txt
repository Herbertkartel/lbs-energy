Business model:
LBS Energy uses a demand-first, partner-led model. It identifies customer needs, scopes projects, obtains supplier quotations and coordinates qualified installation partners. Equipment is sourced against confirmed customer projects rather than held as inventory. Revenue may be earned through agreed equipment supply margins, project development/coordination fees, installation coordination and after-sales/maintenance arrangements. The exact commercial arrangement should be disclosed in each quotation.
LBS Energy is not a lender. Any asset finance, BNPL or flexible payment arrangement is offered by an independent finance partner, where available and subject to that partner's assessment and terms. Where the partner's model allows, it pays the equipment supplier directly; LBS earns only the agreed project margin or service/referral fees.
